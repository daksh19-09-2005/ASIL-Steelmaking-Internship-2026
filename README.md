# 🏭 Aarti Steel International Limited: Summer Internship Reports

## Overview
Detailed technical reports from a 2-month summer technical internship at Aarti Steel International Limited, Ludhiana. This repository covers the complete secondary steelmaking route, wet slag chemistry, hot rolling mill operations, and macrostructural defect analysis in rolled products.

**Author:** Daksh Garg (2023MMB1410)  
**Program:** Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Ropar  
**Date:** June 2026  

## Repository Contents

*   **Report 1: Complete Steel Making Technical Report**
    *   Covers scrap handling and melting in the Electric Arc Furnace (EAF)[cite: 2].
    *   Details secondary refining in the Ladle Refining Furnace (LRF) and Vacuum Degassing (VD) stages[cite: 2].
    *   Outlines the Continuous Casting Machine (CCM) process, including tundish geometry, mould oscillation, and solidification zones[cite: 2].

*   **Report 2: Wet Analysis of EAF, LRF, and VD Slag**
    *   Documents the wet chemical extraction procedures used to determine the exact percentage of main oxides, including SiO2, CaO, MgO, MnO, and FeO[cite: 1].
    *   Includes basicity (V-ratio) calculations and correlates them with the desulfurization and dephosphorization capabilities of the heat[cite: 1].
    *   Explains physical slag color changes from black in the EAF to dead white in the VD stage[cite: 1].

*   **Report 3: Rolling Mill Operations and Quality Testing**
    *   Details the hot rolling process for steel bars through roughing, intermediate, and finishing mills[cite: 4, 5].
    *   Outlines the comprehensive quality assurance testing chain[cite: 4, 5].
    *   Covers Positive Material Identification (PMI), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI), and Automated Ultrasonic Testing (Auto UT) protocols[cite: 4, 5].

*   **Report 4: Macrostructural Defect Analysis**
    *   Investigates surface and subsurface cracks in rolled steel products, specifically Vanadium micro-alloyed steel (Grade 38MnVS6) and Grade 080A47[cite: 3].
    *   Utilizes metallographic preparation, stereo microscopy, and Nital etching to analyze equiaxed grain structures and crack depths[cite: 3].
    *   Tracks centerline segregation using PMI to compare elemental compositions before and after surface grinding[cite: 3].
