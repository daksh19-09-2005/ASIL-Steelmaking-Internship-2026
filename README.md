**Author:** Daksh Garg (Entry No: 2023MMB1410)  
**Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, B.Tech in Metallurgical and Materials Engineering  
**Company:** Aarti Steel International Limited (ASIL), Ludhiana

## Overview
This repository contains comprehensive technical reports and analytical data compiled during my 2-month summer industrial internship at Aarti Steel International Limited. The project covers the end-to-end steel production route, from scrap melting in the Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) to the continuous casting of billets, hot rolling operations, and advanced metallurgical defect analysis.

## Repository Contents

### 1. Primary & Secondary Steelmaking
Detailed process mapping of the EAF, Ladle Refining Furnace (LRF), and Vacuum Degassing (VD) stages.[cite: 1]
*   **EAF Operations:** Scrap handling, arc generation, foaming slag practices, and eccentric bottom tapping (EBT).[cite: 1]
*   **Secondary Refining:** Chemistry control, argon purging, aluminium/CaSi wire feeding, and vacuum treatment for hydrogen/nitrogen removal.[cite: 1]
*   **Continuous Casting:** Tundish metallurgy, mould oscillation, and electromagnetic stirring (EMS) parameters.[cite: 1]

### 2. Wet Chemical Analysis of Slag
Quantitative wet chemical analysis performed on EAF, LRF, and VD slag samples for steel grades EN353 and 40CrMo4.[cite: 2]
*   Determined the exact percentage of major oxides (SiO2, CaO, MgO, FeO, Al2O3) through gravimetric analysis and titration.[cite: 2]
*   Calculated slag basicity (V-ratio) to evaluate desulphurization and dephosphorization efficiency across the refining stages.[cite: 2]

### 3. Hot Rolling & Quality Assurance
Analysis of the hot rolling process and post-rolling non-destructive testing.[cite: 3]
*   **Manufacturing Process:** Reheating furnace operations, high-pressure descaling, and roughing/finishing mill configurations.[cite: 3]
*   **Defect Identification:** Visual inspection for laps, seams, bursting, and rolled-in defects.[cite: 3]
*   **Quality Testing:** Positive Material Identification (PMI) via XRF, Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI), Automated Ultrasonic Testing (Auto UT), and Jominy End-Quench hardenability testing.[cite: 3]

### 4. Macrostructural Defect Investigation
A root-cause metallurgical investigation into surface and sub-surface cracks in rolled steel products (Grades 080A47 and 38MnVS6).[cite: 4]
*   **Chemical Profiling:** Used handheld XRF (PMI) to compare manganese (Mn) enrichment at defect versus non-defect locations, before and after surface grinding.[cite: 4]
*   **Metallography:** Conducted cross-sectional optical microscopy and Nital etching to measure crack depth (e.g., ~240 µm) and length, correlating findings to centerline segregation during continuous casting.[cite: 4]

### 5. Comprehensive Manufacturing & QA Standards
An extended, 37-page master report detailing the complete manufacturing lifecycle and laboratory acceptance criteria for rolled bars.[cite: 5]
*   **Process Control:** Details continuous monitoring parameters including billet discharge temperatures, descaling pressure, and cooling bed residence times.[cite: 5]
*   **Advanced Lab Testing:** Covers Brinell/Rockwell hardness testing, Jominy End-Quench hardenability, and microscopic non-metallic inclusion rating (ASTM E45/EN 10247 standards).[cite: 5]
*   **Defect Reconditioning:** Outlines standard operating procedures for chalk marking, grinding, and reinspection of surface discontinuities.[cite: 5]
